## Retained controls

- Journal: open `journal.html?edit=1` to access **LAYOUT / EDIT**, optional text fields, layout JSON and draft export. These controls stay hidden on the public page.
- Gallery: open `gallery.html?edit=1` to expose the existing grid settings.
- Archive: open `index.html?edit=1#archive` for live flip, gesture, size, movement, typography, spacing, colour, underline, contact and drawing controls. **Preview flip** turns the card. Move the mouse over the reverse face to draw behind its links. Drawing defaults to a black, 2 px, single-pass sketch that redraws at 8 fps and lasts six seconds. Use **Drawing controls** to jump to colour, width, opacity, wiggle amount, frequency, bend length, complexity, frame rate, sketch passes, point spacing, trail lifetime and point limit; **Clear drawing** erases the canvas. Wiggle amount controls displacement; lower frequency gives slower shakes, longer bend length gives broader curves, and higher complexity adds fine irregular detail. Frequency is independent of the drawing frame rate. Sliders are suggested ranges only: numeric boxes accept and save any finite value, including fractions and values beyond the slider endpoints. A frequency of zero freezes the wiggle; a frame rate of zero freezes motion while timed trails still expire. A lifetime of zero keeps strokes until cleared or the page reloads; the point limit discards the oldest marks. Touch retains scrolling and swipe gestures, and reduced-motion visits get still strokes. Drawing animation stops while the archive or tab is hidden. Experiments save only for edit-mode visits in this browser; **Copy values** exports JSON and **Reset defaults** restores the defaults in `assets/js/archive-settings.js`. Public visits use those defaults. **Text depth & shadow** jumps to text lift (slider guide 0–12 px), shadow opacity and softness. The current default is a 23 px text lift, 26% shadow opacity and 0.75 px softness. **Lighting controls** sets mouse follow amount, smoothing, horizontal/vertical cursor offsets, and depth towards the viewer. The light follows the pointer in view space and uses zero cursor offsets, with 170 ms smoothing and 920 px light depth. Zero smoothing follows immediately; zero offsets place it at the cursor. The position indicator is optional, hidden by default, and appears only in edit mode. Fixed X/Y controls, dragging and manual shadow offset have been removed. Each animation frame projects the raised glyphs onto the canvas using its current full 3D pose, so shadows respond to both rotation and mouse movement. Without a mouse (such as on touch screens), the light rests at the viewport centre. **Stroke overspill and fade** sets a 500 px feather outside the red edge. Drawing uses its own transparent sibling plane, padded by at least 128 px and expanded further for large strokes. Pointer tracking continues beyond the red face, including fast exits, so paths extend through the fade instead of ending inside the card. Only the ink is composited; the face does not clip the drawing layer. **Stroke shadow** adds an optional raised ink plane with its own lift, opacity and softness, driven by the same mouse-following light. It is enabled by default with an 8 px ink lift, 26% shadow opacity and 0.75 px softness; it projects a separate black alpha copy of the ink onto the red face. The ink redraws at the selected drawing frame rate while its shadow projection tracks the light and rotation every animation frame. The reverse face preserves 3D depth; its separate text layer handles compact-screen scrolling. Settings use storage key `xavier-archive-settings-v8` so older experiments cannot override the supplied defaults.
